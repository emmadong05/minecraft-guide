Getting Started

This section covers early survival basics: gathering wood, crafting tools, mining core ores, and smelting materials so you can safely progress through your first nights.

  1. 1. Gather wood: find nearby trees, break logs, and collect roughly 10 to 15 logs.

  2. 2. Open inventory with E and convert one log into wood planks.

  3. 3. Craft a crafting table using four planks, move it to your hotbar, place it, and open it.

  4. 4. Craft basic starter items: sticks, a wooden pickaxe, a wooden axe, and a wooden sword.

  5. 5. Find exposed stone or dig down a few blocks, then mine cobblestone with your wooden pickaxe.

  6. 6. Upgrade to stone tools by using the same recipe patterns, replacing planks with cobblestone.

  7. 7. Mine coal first, then mine iron ore once you have at least a stone pickaxe.

  8. 8. Craft a furnace using eight cobblestone and place it near your crafting table.

  9. 9. Smelt ore by placing ore in the top slot and coal in the bottom slot until ingots appear.

  10. 10. Use your new materials to craft torches, shield, and early armor so you can survive night and cave runs.
