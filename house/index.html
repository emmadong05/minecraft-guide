House Guide

Build a compact starter house using the layout from your guide, then furnish it with key utility blocks.

  1. 1. Find a flat 4 by 5 area of ground for your foundation.

  2. 2. Mark out the foundation layout using temporary blocks.

  3. 3. On each marked black square, place three cherry logs vertically to make pillars.

  4. 4. Build a 4 by 5 cherry slab platform on top of the pillars for the roof layer.

  5. 5. Place a cherry door between the two front pillars that are one block apart.

  6. 6. Aim at the inner side of the roof edge blocks to prep stair orientation.

  7. 7. Place pale oak stairs around the roof in a full loop.

  8. 8. Place pale oak planks in a loop around the bottom of the house.

  9. 9. Fill the back wall gaps with pale oak planks.

  10. 10. Fill front corner wall gaps with glass panes.

  11. 11. Fill left wall gaps with glass panes to complete the shell.

  12. 12. Break the six interior floor blocks.

  13. 13. Place the bed in the far-right area inside the house.

  14. 14. Fill the 2 by 2 floor hole with cherry planks.

  15. 15. Place a furnace and crafting table in the left back corner.

  16. 16. Place three barrels along the back wall directly under the roof.

  17. 17. Place a lantern on the back wall for interior lighting.
