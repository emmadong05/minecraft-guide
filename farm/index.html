Farm Guide

This section uses a simple melon and pumpkin farm layout that is easy to build, scale, and maintain for reliable food production.

  1. 1. Find a flat area near your house and clear a 5 by 9 space.

  2. 2. Dig a 1-block-wide, 9-block-long trench down the center.

  3. 3. Fill the center trench with water to hydrate nearby farmland.

  4. 4. Till the dirt rows directly next to water on both sides using any hoe.

  5. 5. Keep the next outer rows as plain dirt or grass for fruit growth blocks.

  6. 6. Plant melon and pumpkin seeds on the tilled farmland rows.

  7. 7. Place border blocks around the outside to contain farm growth area.

  8. 8. Harvest melons and pumpkins when mature, and leave stems intact for regrowth.
