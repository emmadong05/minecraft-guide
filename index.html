Introduction

Minecraft can be quite overwhelming at first with no prior experience. With nearly two decades of updates, there are countless ways to play the game. However, learning and understanding the core concepts of Minecraft will greatly help in determining how you want to play. This introductory guide will teach you the basics on how to survive and progress, as well as open the possibilities in self-discovery.
